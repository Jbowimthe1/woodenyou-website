Our workshop

Made by hand in Newcastle, Oklahoma.

WoodenYou is a family-run woodworking shop built around a simple promise: make pieces that look good, work well, and deserve to be kept. We work with real hardwood, practical joinery and carefully finished details.

The work is the story.

We cut, shape, assemble, sand and finish our products in our Oklahoma shop. Depending on the job, that can mean hand-fitting a serving tray, laminating maple, walnut and cherry for a cutting board, or using a CNC router for precision carving and engraving.

We don't expect customers to trust a stock picture or a vague description. Our gallery shows actual WoodenYou pieces with photographs and posted prices.
