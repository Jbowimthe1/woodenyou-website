Custom Work

Precision where it matters. Craftsmanship everywhere else.

From architectural beams and fireplace mantels to one-of-a-kind statement pieces, every project starts with an idea and ends with something built to last.

1. Share the idea

Send dimensions, inspiration photos, wood preferences, and where the piece will live. Rough is fine. We can refine from there.

2. Shape the build

We determine design direction, materials, sizing, and whether the project leans more traditional, CNC-driven, or a blend of both.

3. Build it right

Once approved, the project moves into production with the same standards you see across the rest of the brand.

What WoodenYou builds

Floating shelves, premium wall art, carved signage, crest work, statement flags, cabinet fronts, custom panels, and select one-off projects.

Not every piece belongs on a CNC. Not every job belongs in hand tools alone. The method follows the build.

What makes it different

The look is premium, but the backbone is practical. Real joinery. Real shop work. Real finishing. Built to feel substantial, not disposable.
