Melted Edge Cutting Board

A substantial maple, cherry and walnut butcher-block board that appears to melt right over the edge of the countertop.

Handcrafted in Newcastle, Oklahoma

Built like a butcher block. Designed to defy gravity.

The striped hardwood glue-up continues directly through the sculpted front edge, making the maple, cherry and walnut appear to flow over the counter instead of stopping at it.

At approximately 18 11/16″ × 22 3/4″ × 1.25″ thick, this is a full-size working cutting board with the mass and presence of a premium butcher block.

Details: maple, cherry and walnut hardwood; routed juice groove; raised metal feet for stability, clearance and a clean floating shadow line; hand-finished and oiled.

Every glue-up and piece of hardwood is different, so every Melted Edge board will have its own stripe placement, grain and character.
